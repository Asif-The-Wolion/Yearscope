About the data

Weather and rainfall come from Open-Meteo (forecast & historical archive APIs). Sunrise, sunset, twilight and daylight duration are calculated locally from standard solar-position astronomy — not fetched from a third party.

Season boundaries and the monsoon window are estimates derived from several years of historical data at your location. They describe typical patterns, not a guarantee for any single year.

YearScope is free, has no account, no ads, and sends only your chosen location's coordinates to Open-Meteo's public APIs to fetch weather data. Nothing else is transmitted anywhere.
